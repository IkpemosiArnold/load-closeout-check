Checking

Ready means the rate con, invoice and POD are all here with one load number, the invoice comes from the carrier and MC on the rate con, the POD is signed, dated and clean, every charge was agreed on the rate con and is supported, and the lines add up. A charge billed twice, a line it cannot read or a remit-to line holds the load. Anything held back comes with the reason and the lines it looked at.

Detention is worked out from the POD: the clock starts at the later of the appointment and the truck's arrival, stops at the out time, less the free hours, in quarter hours. A lumper needs its receipt.
